Restaurant

From Our Greenhouse to Your Table

Dining at Ura e Kovaçit follows the rhythm of the seasons. Our kitchen cooks with vegetables picked from our own greenhouse and garden, paired with fresh meats, dairy, and produce from neighboring farms around Voskopojë and Korçë.

You will find traditional regional dishes like wood-fired lakror alongside Mediterranean favorites, as well as vegetarian and vegan plates prepared with the same care as our house specialties.

Mornings start with a generous homemade breakfast of fresh-baked bread and local jams, and evenings invite you to linger over a glass of Albanian wine beside the fire.

Experiences

Discover Ura e Kovaçit

Whether you feel like riding through the meadows, walking into the pine forest, or visiting Voskopojë’s historic churches just a few minutes away, there is plenty to discover at your own pace.

Forest & River Walks

Step straight from the grounds onto pine-shaded trails or stroll beside the river. In winter, those same paths turn into a quiet snowy landscape with skiing nearby.

Horseback Adventures

Explore open meadows, forest tracks, and hillside paths on horseback, with guided rides suited to both first-timers and experienced riders.

Agrotourism & Greenhouse

Walk through our greenhouse and gardens, say hello to the horses and farm animals, and browse homemade local products on the shelf at reception.

Albanian Wine Tastings

Sample standout Albanian wines from local producers, whether paired with your dinner in the lodge or enjoyed during a relaxed tasting.

Guests also enjoy buggy rides into the village, riverside fishing, a children’s play area with rabbits and horses, outdoor fire pits with roasted chestnuts, and evenings of live music.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ura e Kovaçit located?

Ura e Kovaçit is at Rruga nacionale Km 1, at the entrance to Voskopojë village in Korçë County, Albania, beside the Voskopoja river and surrounded by black pine forest. It is about a 30-minute drive from Korçë and just minutes from Voskopojë’s historic churches and hiking trails.

Which rooms have a fireplace, bathtub, or jacuzzi?

Our Superior Rooms feature either a fireplace or a freestanding bathtub, and our Deluxe Rooms combine a private fireplace with a freestanding bathtub or jacuzzi. Duplex, Triple, and Suite rooms offer the most space for families and groups. All rooms have heating and air conditioning.

Does the restaurant serve vegetarian and vegan food?

Yes. Our restaurant serves traditional Voskopojë and Albanian dishes alongside European and Mediterranean cuisine, made with organic produce from our own greenhouse and garden and from neighboring farms. Vegetarian and vegan dishes are prepared with the same care as our signature specialties.

What is served at breakfast?

We serve a generous homemade breakfast made with fresh local ingredients—guests especially love our warm oven-baked bread, house-made jams, and traditional regional specialties.

Do you offer massages and wellness treatments?

Yes. Our professional therapists, including specialists in traditional Thai therapeutic massage, offer treatments in our dedicated wellness space or in the comfort of your room. You can book a treatment by WhatsApp at +355 68 909 8589.

What activities are available?

Guests enjoy horseback riding, guided and self-guided hikes through the pine forests, walks along the river, visits to our farm and greenhouse, Albanian wine tastings, outdoor fire pits, and live music evenings. In winter the area is covered in snow and there is skiing nearby.

Is Ura e Kovaçit good for families with children?

Yes. Families can choose spacious Duplex, Triple, and Suite rooms, and children love the play area, the horses, rabbits, and other farm animals, and the open green spaces around the property.

Can I host a wedding, event, or retreat?

Yes. With its stone chapel, landscaped grounds, and mountain setting, Ura e Kovaçit hosts weddings, blessings, celebrations, and corporate or wellness retreats. Contact us by WhatsApp or email to plan your event.

Is there parking and an EV charger?

Yes. On-site parking and an electric vehicle charger are available for guests.

How do I book a room?

You can check availability and book instantly online in the Reservations section of this page, or contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp at +355 68 909 8589 or by email at info@uraekovacit.com for a personalized booking.
